How it's built.

[ 02 — Method ]

Signal

LightGBM ensemble (3 seeds × 21d cross-sectional rank target). 24 features — 20 stock-level alphas plus 4 regime z-scores — exponentially recency-weighted, half-life 252 trading days.

Backtest

Per-market top-quintile, signal-weighted within each market, equal-weight across US/MY/HK. EMA-smoothed signal, vol-targeted 10%, leverage band [0.03, 1.0]. Long-only.

Sizing

Per-name Kelly: wi ∝ μi / σi², fractional, capped per position, redistributed to fill the budget. Adapts to the asset's own characteristics — not realised P&L.
